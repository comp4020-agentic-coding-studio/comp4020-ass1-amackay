# Assignment 1

## The breakthrough

From previous crits, I was quietly frustrated about some of Claude Code's behaviour that I repeatedly had to correct - stuff like inferring my rationale and stating it as fact, or making projects insufficiently self-contained. I took a step back, reflected to figure out what the general problem was, and changed my system-level CLAUDE.md to provide better instructions to get the behaviour I want in general.

## What it changed about who I want to be

I've been increasingly hands-off and high-level when providing Claude with directions. But for getting minor details exactly how I want them, it is useful to actually have some understanding of the code structure, to know what changes will be cheap and easy. So I'm starting to see first-hand personal evidence of what other people might've told me is obvious - it's important to still keep up my own programming skills and be able to navigate and review code myself.
